# The Lantern of Wenlow
*The Wenlow Chronicles, Chapter One*

An original, old-school tap-to-explore quest adventure that plays in any web browser and installs like an app on Android, iPhone, tablets and PCs.

**Play:** https://froghaul.github.io/lantern-of-wenlow/

## The story
On Midsummer night the Lantern of Dawn atop the Grey Tower went dark, and King Aldren of Wenlow fell into an enchanted sleep. As a young knight, you must gather moon-oil, a starlight wick and the sunstone flint, get past the Grey Warden and light the Lantern again.

## Install it on a phone
- **Android (Chrome):** open the link, tap ⋮ then **Add to Home screen** / **Install app**.
- **iPhone (Safari):** open the link, tap Share then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **PC (Chrome/Edge):** click the install icon in the address bar.

Once it's installed, the game works offline and saves your progress automatically.

## How to play
- Tap the ground to walk your knight there.
- Tap people and things: your knight walks over, then you choose to look, talk or take.
- Tap an item in your pack, then tap something in the scene to use it there.
- In fights, guard HIGH or LOW when the red arrows show, then STRIKE while your foe is off balance.
- Stuck? Open ☰ and turn on **Show tappable spots**.

## For developers
- `dev/playtest.html`: open in Chrome to play the whole game automatically (the log shows each step and the final score).
- `dev/walk-debug.html`: every scene with its walkable ground and stand points drawn on.

## About
Everything in this game (story, characters, places, artwork and sound) is original. The art is drawn with SVG shapes in code and the sounds are synthesised, so there are no image or audio files. Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, no build step.
